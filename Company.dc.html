Structure & ownership

A phased approach to ownership.

LEVELUP begins as a sole trader, giving the founder complete ownership and control, faster decisions and less administrative overhead. As the business grows and seeks external investment, it's positioned to transition into a proprietary limited company (Pty Ltd) for limited liability protection and greater opportunities for expansion, partnerships and capital.

Legal & data protection

Handling sensitive data responsibly.

LEVELUP collects personal and health-related information, so compliance with Australian privacy legislation is essential. The business adheres to the Privacy Act 1988 and implements appropriate safeguards to protect customer data, and complies with the Australian Consumer Law governing consumer rights, product guarantees and advertising practices.

Some features, such as AI-assisted form checking, may use device camera footage. Camera footage is treated as sensitive personal information: it is processed only for the feature it supports, handled under the same Privacy Act safeguards, and never used or shared beyond that purpose without consent.
