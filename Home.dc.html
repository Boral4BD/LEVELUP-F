The problem

Motivation shouldn't be the hardest part of getting fit.

Physical inactivity among teenagers and young adults is a growing issue in Australia. Most people don't stay active because it's hard to stay motivated, gym memberships are expensive, and the fitness apps on offer track workouts without making them enjoyable.

Barrier 01

Low motivation

Without a reason to keep going, exercise habits fall apart within weeks.

Barrier 02

Costly memberships

Traditional gyms price out the people who'd benefit most from staying active.

Barrier 03

Uninspiring apps

Most fitness apps just log workouts — they don't make you want to open them again.

The insight

People want fitness that feels like a game.

Market research conducted during LEVELUP's development found that most respondents wanted competition, rewards and progression systems like the ones they already love in video games — a clear gap in a fitness market built for tracking, not engagement.
